# Organic social content calendar best practices

Use these rules for Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn posts in the Content Creator workspace.

## Voice

- Write feed-native copy, not landing pages, blog intros, or ad spec sheets.
- One clear angle per post. Lead with a hook that states a concrete outcome or next step.
- Plain language. No jargon dumps (SEO pipeline, actionable tasks, dashboard, etc.).
- Agency voice: speak as we/our/us when the site is an agency, not anonymous tips.

## Strategy brief first

- Plan angle, hook, CTA, and hashtags before writing platform copy.
- captionHook is sentence 1 of the Instagram/Facebook caption. Copy agents expand it only.
- Do not paste the raw keyword into hooks or captions. Rewrite into natural English.

## Instagram and Facebook (shared field)

- Instagram rules are strictest. Optimize for Instagram even when cross-posting to Facebook.
- Caption body: hook sentence plus at most one short support line.
- Target 125 characters for the hook (visible before "more"). Hard max 300 characters total including CTA.
- CTA: link in bio, tap the link, or read more. Do not paste long URLs in the caption body.
- Hashtags: 3 to 5 on the last line. No hashtag walls.
- No emoji chains. Zero or one emoji max.
- No bullet lists or multiple paragraphs.

## LinkedIn

- Professional, authoritative tone. 2 to 3 short paragraphs max.
- Target 900 characters. Hard max 1300 characters.
- Optional 0 to 3 hashtags at the end. No emoji spam.
- One clear CTA. Can mention the landing page topic without a long URL.

## Events column

- Never reference holidays, seasons, observances, or event hooks unless Event context is explicitly provided in the user payload.
- Do not infer events from the scheduled date alone.

## Focus keyword

- Keyword is a research seed. Rewrite into natural phrasing with correct grammar.
- Never jam the raw keyword string into captions or hooks.
