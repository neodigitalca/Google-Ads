# NEO Pulse program brief

## Product

NEO Pulse is an AI content and flow manager for WordPress SEO teams and agencies.

- **Product URL:** https://neodigital.ca/neo-pulse
- **Agency demo CTA:** https://neodigital.ca/
- **Core value:** Search Console signals become short action lists tied to WordPress URLs.
- **Audience:** WordPress SEO agencies, in-house content ops, local and programmatic SEO teams.

## Program modules

- **Overview:** Sitemap, page inventory, meta optimization, and content research tied to live WordPress URLs.
- **Bulk generator:** CSV and prompt bulk flows with approved templates and one brief per page.
- **Manager:** Properties, API keys, master rules, AI models, Google services, teams, tasks, and chat.
- **PPC:** Google Search and Meta feed ad workspaces grounded in WordPress page context and GSC queries.
- **Integrations:** WordPress plugin connects briefs, status, and reporting to client pages and posts.

## Visual language (Instagram ads)

- **Primary output:** Designed Instagram feed or story sponsored-post creative with bold type hierarchy and visible focal graphic elements (icons, shapes, accent bars, motifs).
- **Accent:** `#84BC00` lime green when it fits the brief `visualVibe`.
- **Background:** From creative brief `backgroundTreatment`. Not geo-keyword driven.
- **Typography:** Lato. No logos in generated creatives.
- **On-image copy:** Exact headline and optional subline from the creative brief only.
- **Tool palette:** Follow brief `visualToolPalette` (degree per tool). UI or device vignettes only when `device_screen.degree > 0`. Device screens use context-driven realistic WordPress or page-builder layouts with placeholder blocks only, not abstract chart UI.
- **Finished creative only:** Never paint design-spec labels ("Designed", placement, aspect ratio, "Sponsored Ad") into the image.
