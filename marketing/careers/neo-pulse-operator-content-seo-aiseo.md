## About Neo Digital

Neo Digital is building **NEO Pulse**, an AI content and flow manager for WordPress SEO teams and agencies. The platform turns Search Console signals into short, URL-level action lists tied to live WordPress pages and posts.

We serve WordPress SEO agencies, in-house content operations, and local/programmatic SEO teams who need to move faster without sacrificing quality.

This is an early operator role. You will run client SEO production on NEO Pulse while the product is still evolving, and help us harden workflows before we expand the team.

---

## About the Role

We are looking for a **hands-on Content SEO specialist with AISEO experience** to operate and maintain NEO Pulse day to day.

This is not a strategy-only or reporting-only position. You will use the platform daily to optimize live client sites, run AI-assisted audits and content production, maintain WordPress SEO hygiene, and flag what breaks or needs improvement as we ship new features.

You bring heavy traditional SEO knowledge, working technical SEO skills, and practical experience using AI in production workflows (with strong QA instincts).

---

## What You Will Own

### Daily SEO operations

- Turn **Google Search Console** performance data into prioritized URL-level action lists
- Run the **Overview / Content Optimizer** workflow: sitemap inventory, meta optimization, on-page content updates, FAQ/header/link work, and upload back to WordPress
- Execute **bulk content pipelines** (CSV, prompt, and import flows) with approved templates and one brief per page
- Maintain **entity / programmatic / local page** generation where clients need scale (SAP-style clusters, location pages)
- Run **sitemap maintenance**: cluster and merge overlapping URLs, legacy redirects, URL optimization plans
- Produce **client reporting** from GSC month-over-month data and AI-assisted report sections
- Support **local SEO** with Maps grid rank tracking and Google Business Profile post workflows where applicable

### AI SEO (AISEO) and audits

- Run **AI-assisted SEO briefs** grounded in SERP data, GSC, and competitive context
- Operate **AI meta and content optimization** (titles, descriptions, focus keywords, headers, FAQs, internal links) with human QA before publish
- Conduct **content gap analysis**, post-library grading, and proposal/site audit workflows
- Tune **Master Rules** and per-client AI instructions so output matches brand, E-E-A-T, and search intent
- Review AI output for accuracy, hallucination risk, over-optimization, and duplicate or thin content patterns
- Help define what "good" looks like for AI audits as the platform matures

### Technical SEO and platform maintenance

- Manage **WordPress property connections** and verify inventory sync, publish paths, and SEO meta application
- Monitor and maintain client-side **NEO Pulse WP** modules: redirects, image SEO, speed/cache, script manager, sitemap/robots
- Work with **DataForSEO**, **Semrush**, and integrated research tools for SERP dumps, competitor research, and audit context
- Configure and maintain integrations: **OpenRouter** (LLM), **Google Analytics**, **Search Console**, **Google Business Profile**
- Track bulk job progress, troubleshoot failed runs, and document repeatable fixes
- Coordinate with the product builder on bugs, UX friction, and operator workflow gaps

### Collaboration and delivery

- Use **Teams** (chat, tasks, notes) for client work coordination
- Deliver research outputs where needed: proposals, citations, backlinking research
- Optionally support **PPC creative** and **social/GBP** modules grounded in live page context

## What We're Looking For

### Must-have

**Traditional SEO (heavy)**

- 3+ years hands-on content SEO for real sites (agency or in-house)
- Strong command of on-page SEO, search intent, internal linking, cannibalization, and content refresh strategy
- Comfortable reading GSC performance, indexing, and query/page relationships
- Experience with WordPress content operations at scale (not just publishing blog posts)
- Client reporting literacy: what changed, why, and what to do next

**Technical SEO (working knowledge)**

- URL architecture, redirects, sitemap hygiene, crawl/index issues
- Schema and FAQ patterns, image SEO, page speed basics
- Ability to diagnose why an optimization did or did not stick on WordPress

**AI SEO / AISEO (practical, not theoretical)**

- Experience using LLMs in production SEO workflows (briefs, meta, content refresh, audits)
- Strong editorial judgment: you know when AI output is wrong, generic, or risky
- Understanding of E-E-A-T, entity coverage, and how AI content should be governed
- Comfort with prompt shaping and QA loops, not "prompt and pray"

**Operator mindset**

- Self-directed remote worker who can run a stack without hand-holding
- Detail-oriented: bulk workflows fail silently if you are not checking outputs
- Willing to operate beta software and document issues clearly

### Strong plus

- Local SEO or programmatic SEO experience
- Semrush Site Audit or DataForSEO familiarity
- Google Business Profile management
- Elementor or page-builder content workflows
- Basic HTML/Markdown cleanup for AI-generated content
- Experience with SEO agencies serving SMB clients (home services, trades, professional services, etc.)

### Not required (helpful long-term)

- React/TypeScript development
- PHP/WordPress plugin development
- MCP/agent tooling (Cursor, Claude tools against WordPress APIs)
