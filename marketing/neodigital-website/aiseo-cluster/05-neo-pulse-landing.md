# NEO Pulse

**NEO Pulse** is the AI content and flow manager for WordPress SEO teams. Search Console signals become a short action list tied to real URLs: titles to fix, pages to refresh, tickets ready to ship.

Neo Digital built it to run [AISEO](https://neodigital.ca/aiseo/) on connected sites. Your plan, briefs, and publishes stay on the same pages and posts you already have.

[Book a demo](https://neodigital.ca/contact/)

## The problem it solves

Your SEO plan lives in a spreadsheet. Your content lives in WordPress. They never match.

NEO Pulse keeps strategy, briefs, status, and reporting on live and draft URLs. Agencies and in-house teams stop exporting “recommendations” that nobody implements.

## Who it is for

- **WordPress SEO agencies** that need bulk templates, QA rules, and one brief per page
- **In-house content ops** that need a weekly GSC list, not another dashboard
- **Local and programmatic teams** that ship location and service pages without duplicate fluff

If you want Neo Digital to operate the loop for you, that is the [AISEO service](https://neodigital.ca/aiseo/). If you want your team to operate it, that is NEO Pulse.

## What you get

### Overview (the AISEO workspace)

Sitemap inventory, page and post grids, meta optimization, and content research on the URLs Google already sees.

AISEO actions on selected rows include research, keywords, all meta (titles, descriptions, slugs), FAQs, content, headers, cleanup, internal links, Wikipedia/entity links, answer, overview, scenario, and in-content images.

### Bulk generator

CSV, prompt, and import flows with approved templates. Scale service-area and entity pages without a second CMS.

### Manager

Properties, API keys, master rules, models, Google services, teams, tasks, and chat. Operators work in one place.

### Reporting

Search Console month-over-month at URL level: what changed after the last pass.

### WordPress plugin (NEO Pulse WP)

Editor-side tools, redirects, image SEO, speed/cache, sitemap/robots, and backend assist so publish is not a copy-paste into a disconnected builder.

### PPC (when you need ads from the same URLs)

Google Search and Meta workspaces grounded in page context and GSC queries, so paid copy does not drift from what the site says.

## How the weekly loop works

1. Connect WordPress and Search Console.
2. Pull inventory. See pages, posts, and entity/service-area URLs in one grid.
3. Sort by the metric that slipped (clicks, CTR, position).
4. Run AISEO on the short list. QA. Publish.
5. Report. Repeat.

No parallel “AI blog.” The site is the system of record.

## FAQ

### Is NEO Pulse a plugin or a web app?

Both. The app is the workspace (overview, generator, reporting, teams). NEO Pulse WP is the WordPress plugin that connects inventory, editor tools, and publish.

### Do I have to hire Neo Digital to use it?

No. Agencies and in-house teams can run NEO Pulse themselves. Neo Digital also sells [AISEO](https://neodigital.ca/aiseo/) as a done-for-you path using the same product.

### Does it work without Search Console?

You can connect WordPress and still optimize inventory. Priority is worse without GSC. The core promise is signals to URL actions.

### Is this an AI writer?

It is a production system. Writing is one action among research, meta, links, QA, and publish. An AI writer does not own your sitemap.
