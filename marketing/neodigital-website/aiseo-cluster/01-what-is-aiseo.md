# What Is AISEO?

**AISEO** is the short form of **AI SEO**. It is search engine optimization that uses large language models for research, briefs, meta, and on-page drafts, then holds those drafts to the same standard as any other SEO work: search intent, facts, uniqueness, and a measurable URL.

Neo Digital uses AISEO as the name of that practice. The [AISEO service page](https://neodigital.ca/aiseo/) is the commercial hub. This page is the definition.

## The short answer

AI SEO is not “write a blog with ChatGPT.” It is a loop:

1. Pick a URL that Search Console or the SERP says is underperforming or missing.
2. Build a brief from queries, entities, and first-party facts (what the business can actually claim).
3. Let a model draft against that brief.
4. Reject or edit anything generic, invented, or off-intent.
5. Publish to the same WordPress URL.
6. Watch clicks, CTR, and position on that URL.

If a step is missing, you do not have AISEO. You have content volume.

## What AISEO includes

| Included | Not included |
|----------|----------------|
| Keyword and entity research tied to a URL | Keyword lists with no page assignment |
| Titles, descriptions, slugs, headers, FAQs | Unreviewed bulk blogs |
| Body copy with sourced claims | Invented stats and fake case studies |
| Internal links and index hygiene | A second site that never ships to WordPress |
| Human QA against a brief | “The model said it, so we published it” |

The writing pass is documented separately: [AI content optimization](https://neodigital.ca/aiseo/ai-content-optimization/).

## How it differs from traditional SEO

Traditional SEO already had research, on-page, links, and reporting. AISEO does not replace those jobs. It changes who does the first draft and how fast a URL can move from “needs work” to “ready to QA.”

The failure mode is the opposite of old SEO. Old SEO was too slow. Bad AISEO is too fast: hundreds of similar pages, cannibalized clusters, and no Search Console owner.

We design the workflow so speed cannot skip QA. One brief per page. Fail-fast on invented numbers. No silent “if the model missed it, patch it in code” attitude on client sites. Fix the brief.

## How it differs from GEO and AEO

**Generative engine optimization (GEO)** and **answer engine optimization (AEO)** are about being cited in AI Overviews, chat answers, and other generated results. That is a real channel. It is not the whole job.

AISEO still has to win classic organic: title, snippet, ranking, click. GEO sits under that practice. Read [generative engine optimization](https://neodigital.ca/aiseo/generative-engine-optimization/) when the goal is citations in AI answers.

## FAQ

### What is AI SEO in one sentence?

AI SEO is SEO where models draft research and on-page copy for specific URLs, and humans approve what gets published.

### Is AISEO a tool or a service?

Both, depending on the path. Neo Digital sells AISEO as a service. [NEO Pulse](https://neodigital.ca/neo-pulse/) is the WordPress-connected platform that runs the same workflow.

### Will AI SEO replace SEO specialists?

It replaces blank-page drafting. It does not replace judgment: intent, cannibalization, E-E-A-T, and “this claim is not true.” We hire for that judgment.

### Does Google penalize AI content?

Google’s public guidance targets unhelpful, scaled, low-value pages, not “a human used a model.” Thin AI at scale is the risk. Unique, accurate pages that match the query are the job.
