# AI SEO Audit

An **AI SEO audit** is how we start [AISEO](https://neodigital.ca/aiseo/) when you are not ready for a full content sprint. We pull Search Console, WordPress inventory, and SERP context, then return a short action list tied to real URLs.

You do not get a 90-page PDF that nobody implements. You get: titles to fix, pages to refresh, clusters to merge, tickets ready to ship.

## What the audit answers

- Which URLs already get impressions but lose the click (title/meta job)
- Which URLs slipped out of positions 4–10 (content and intent job)
- Which queries have no adequate page (gap)
- Which pages cannibalize each other (sitemap / cluster job)
- What is safe to automate vs what a human must rewrite

That is the same loop [NEO Pulse](https://neodigital.ca/neo-pulse/) runs weekly. The audit is the first pass, done by us.

## What we collect

| Source | Why |
|--------|-----|
| **Google Search Console** | Clicks, CTR, position, query-to-page map |
| **WordPress + sitemap** | Live vs draft, orphans, index bloat |
| **SERP / competitor snapshot** | What currently wins the query |
| **On-page sample** | Titles, headers, thin templates, duplicate blocks |

We work server-side against your properties. You are not asked to paste API keys into a browser tool.

## Deliverable

A prioritized list, URL by URL, in language an editor can execute:

1. **Meta only** (title, description, slug)
2. **On-page** (headers, FAQs, answer, internal links)
3. **Full content** (rebuild against a brief)
4. **Technical / IA** (redirect, merge, noindex, sitemap)

Plus a note on [AI content optimization](https://neodigital.ca/aiseo/ai-content-optimization/) vs structure-only GEO work when AI Overviews matter: [generative engine optimization](https://neodigital.ca/aiseo/generative-engine-optimization/).

## Who it is for

- Site owners who have “done SEO” for years and cannot see what to do this month
- Agencies inheriting a WordPress mess (100+ service pages, no owner)
- Teams considering NEO Pulse who want a human-run sample of the action-list model

Edmonton and remote Canadian clients are the default. WordPress is the native CMS.

## What it is not

- A legal or security audit
- A guarantee of rankings
- A substitute for Google Business Profile work if local pack is the only channel (we will say when Maps, not organic, is the constraint)

## After the audit

Implement with us under AISEO, or load the same properties into NEO Pulse and have your operators run the list. Either path uses one brief per URL and QA before publish.

## FAQ

### How long does an AI SEO audit take?

Depends on URL count and Search Console access. A focused site (dozens of money pages) is a short engagement. A programmatic site (hundreds of URLs) is scoped by cluster, not by reading every page by hand.

### Do you need Search Console?

Yes, for a real audit. Without it we can only guess from the SERP. The product of this service is GSC-to-URL actions.

### Will you use AI to write the audit?

We use models to cluster and draft. Priority and “do not ship this” calls stay with an SEO operator. That is the point of AISEO.

### Can this turn into ongoing work?

Yes. The audit is the sample. Retainers and [NEO Pulse](https://neodigital.ca/neo-pulse/) are how the list gets cleared every month.
