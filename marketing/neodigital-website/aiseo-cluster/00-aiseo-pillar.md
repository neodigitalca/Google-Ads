## What AISEO is

AISEO is search optimization where models do the drafting and research lift, and operators keep the contract: intent, facts, brand, and index hygiene.

It is not a bulk of interchangeable city pages. It is not unreviewed AI blogs. It is URL-level work on a connected WordPress site.

We use AISEO when a page has a job in search (rank, click, answer, convert) and the current draft fails that job. The model writes against a brief. We reject generic output the same way we would reject a junior writer who never opened Search Console.

## How an AISEO engagement runs

1. **Connect the site.** WordPress inventory, sitemap, and Search Console. We work from live and draft URLs, not a disconnected spreadsheet.
2. **Prioritize.** Clicks down, CTR soft, position slipping out of 4–10, or entity pages that never earned impressions. The list is short on purpose.
3. **Brief each URL.** One brief per page: intent, entities, first-party facts, must-keep claims, what to cut.
4. **Generate, then QA.** Titles, copy, FAQs, and links go through editorial review. We fail drafts that invent numbers, skip the query, or read like every other AI page in the niche.
5. **Publish to WordPress.** Meta and body land on the same URLs your customers already hit. No parallel “AI site.”
6. **Report.** Month-over-month Search Console: what changed, which URLs moved, what we do next.

## AISEO vs classic SEO retainers

Classic retainers often produce a PDF and a handful of blogs. AISEO produces URL diffs.

We still do technical hygiene (sitemaps, redirects, cannibalization, schema patterns). We still do local: Google Business Profile aligned with what the site actually says. The change is the production loop. Research and drafts are model-assisted. Publish is still WordPress. Accountability is still page-level Search Console.

If you need to show up in AI Overviews, ChatGPT citations, and answer engines as well as blue links, that is [generative engine optimization](https://neodigital.ca/aiseo/generative-engine-optimization/) as a cluster under this same practice.

## FAQ

### What does AISEO stand for?

AISEO means AI SEO: search optimization where AI drafts and research are governed by SEO operators. At Neo Digital it specifically means work on connected WordPress URLs, not generic chat output.

### Is AI SEO the same as buying an AI writer?

No. An AI writer produces text. AISEO is a production system: inventory, priority, brief, generate, QA, publish, measure. The writing is one step.

### Do you only work on WordPress?

WordPress is the native path. That is where inventory, meta, and publish stay on the same URLs. If your stack is not WordPress, say so on the contact form and we will tell you if the engagement still fits.

### How is this different from generative engine optimization?

GEO is how pages get cited in AI answers and overviews. AISEO is the broader practice (classic rankings plus AI search). We treat GEO as a cluster under AISEO, not a replacement for titles, links, and Search Console.

### Can my agency use this without handing you the site?

Yes. That is [NEO Pulse](https://neodigital.ca/neo-pulse/): your team runs the workflow. Neo Digital AISEO is the done-with-you or done-for-you version of the same loop.
