# AI Content Optimization

AI content optimization is the production heart of [AISEO](https://neodigital.ca/aiseo/). We do not generate a pile of articles in a vacuum. We open a WordPress URL, attach a brief, rewrite what search and the business require, then publish.

This is the page for teams who already have pages (or a bulk of service-area URLs) and need them to rank and convert without sounding like a model dump.

## What we optimize

On a typical URL the pass includes:

- **Title and meta description** that match the query and earn the click
- **Slug** short enough to read, not a headline pasted into the permalink
- **H2/H3 map** that follows the SERP and the decision the searcher is making
- **Answer block** at the top: the actual answer, not a history lesson
- **Body** written as the business, using only claims you can defend
- **FAQs** that match People Also Ask and sales objections
- **Internal links** to money pages and related cluster URLs
- **Cleanup** of duplicate blocks, leftover builder junk, and hollow “our team” lines

That maps to the NEO Pulse AISEO actions: All Meta, Content, Headers, FAQs, Links, Answer, Overview, Scenario, in-content images.

## The brief is the product

A model without a brief copies the SERP and invents confidence. We brief every URL with:

- Primary query and allowed semantic variants (exact phrase is capped, not stuffed)
- Entities the page must cover
- First-party facts: what this company can say (projects, process, service area)
- Forbidden claims: prices, years, volumes, and reviews that are not sourced
- Intent: informational, commercial, or local

If the draft misses a listed fact, the draft is incomplete. We do not “fix it in post” with regex. We fix the brief and regenerate or edit.

## WordPress is the CMS of record

AI SEO for WordPress only works if publish hits the same posts and pages Google already knows.

- Inventory from sitemap and WordPress, not a shadow CMS
- Rank Math / Yoast-style fields updated with the rest of the page
- Draft or live, your choice, but one URL
- Featured and in-content images as part of the same job when the template needs them

Agencies that already live in WordPress do not need a new content silo. They need [NEO Pulse](https://neodigital.ca/neo-pulse/) or Neo Digital operating that loop.

## Quality bar (what we reject)

We reject drafts that:

- Open with a dictionary definition of the keyword
- Repeat the same benefit in Answer, Overview, and three H2s
- Use hollow authority (“trusted experts,” “we pride ourselves”) with no field detail
- Invent CAD/USD figures, install counts, or rebate programs
- Cannibalize a stronger URL in the same cluster

Local and service pages get extra rules: primary city stays consistent, no competitor-city economics pasted in, no “{keyword} {neighborhood} {city}” slug stacks in prose.

## Scale without chaos

When the site needs many service or location URLs, we still use one brief per page. Templates and CSV/prompt bulk exist so structure is shared. Facts are not.

That is how programmatic SEO on WordPress stays indexable. Duplicate fluff hurts the whole domain. If you are not sure which URLs deserve a full rewrite, start with an [AI SEO audit](https://neodigital.ca/aiseo/ai-seo-audit/).

## FAQ

### Can you optimize existing posts, or only new ones?

Existing URLs first. Search Console already tells us which posts get impressions and which waste crawl. New URLs come after the inventory has an owner.

### Do you write in our brand voice?

Yes, when you give rules and examples. Master rules and per-site instructions exist for a reason. Without them, the model writes generic B2B.

### How do you avoid duplicate content on location pages?

Shared template for structure. Unique brief for entities, services, and first-party facts. If two cities have nothing distinct to say, we do not ship two pages.

### Is this the same as spinning articles?

No. Spinning is variation for volume. Optimization is a specific URL vs a specific query, with QA.
