# Generative Engine Optimization

**Generative engine optimization (GEO)** is the work of making your pages easy for AI systems to quote: Google AI Overviews, answer engines, and chat tools that cite the web. It sits inside [AISEO](https://neodigital.ca/aiseo/), not beside it.

If classic SEO asked “will this rank and get the click?”, GEO adds “will a generated answer use this page as a source?”

Related names you will see in the same SERP: **GEO SEO**, **answer engine optimization (AEO)**, **AI search optimization**. We treat them as one cluster. The page still has to be the best source, not a pile of schema tricks.

## Direct answer

GEO is on-page and entity work so machines can lift a clean answer, with attribution, from a URL you control.

That means:

- A direct answer near the top (not a 400-word throat-clear)
- Headings that match real questions
- Facts a model can repeat without hallucinating extra numbers
- Entities, authors, and organization identity that match the rest of the web
- Internal links that show this URL owns the topic

It does not mean stuffing “according to experts” or buying fake citations.

You cannot GEO your way out of a thin site. Generated answers prefer pages that already look like sources: specific, current, attributed. Our [AI content optimization](https://neodigital.ca/aiseo/ai-content-optimization/) pass is how those pages get written.

## What we change for AI search

1. **Answer-first sections.** Each H2 opens with the answer, then the mechanism. Models lift the first clean sentence.
2. **Question-shaped FAQs.** Real questions from Search Console and People Also Ask, not filler.
3. **Defensible numbers.** Units and currency when the fact is sourced. No invented ROI.
4. **Entity coverage.** People, places, products, and Wikipedia-backed concepts where they help disambiguate.
5. **Author and org clarity.** Who wrote it, who the business is, what they actually do in Edmonton and other service areas you name.
6. **Crawlable HTML.** Answers in the page, not only in an image or an accordion the crawler never sees.

Technical SEO still matters: indexation, canonicals, duplicates. An AI Overview cannot cite a noindexed URL.

## What GEO is not

- A guarantee you will appear in AI Overviews. Google does not sell that placement.
- Replacing backlinks, local relevance, or a site that has no topical depth.
- Keyword-stuffing “ChatGPT” and “AI Overview” into every H2.

If the underlying page is weak, we start with an [AI SEO audit](https://neodigital.ca/aiseo/ai-seo-audit/) and a content pass, then GEO structure.

## FAQ

### What is generative engine optimization?

GEO is optimizing pages so generative systems (AI Overviews, answer engines, chat with browsing) can extract and cite an accurate answer from your URL.

### Is GEO the same as AEO?

Close enough for a service page. AEO stresses answer engines. GEO stresses generative engines. We implement one content contract that serves both.

### Can you guarantee AI Overview placement?

No. We can make the page extractable and accurate. Placement is the engine’s choice.

### Do I still need traditional rankings?

Yes. Most AI answers still draw from pages that already have visibility, crawl access, and topical authority.
