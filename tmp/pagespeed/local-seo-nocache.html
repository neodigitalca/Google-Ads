How It Goes

How The Process Works

A local SEO engagement starts with the profile and the pages that should rank in the pack. Then we clean citations, then we publish neighborhood copy. The city-wide program is Edmonton SEO.

Step 01

Profile And Pack Audit

We audit the Google Business Profile, the local pack, and the pages that should own the service queries. You approve the list first.

Step 02

Listings And Pages

We fix categories, services, photos, citations, and the on-page city copy that Maps needs.

Step 03

NAP And Conversion QA

We check Name Address Phone consistency and that calls and forms still fire from the profile.

Step 04

Reviews And Maintain

We keep reviews and posts on a calendar. Broader rankings stay on Edmonton SEO. Paid gaps sit on Google Ads.

Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How is local SEO different from Edmonton SEO?

Local SEO wins maps and near-me searches. Edmonton SEO is the full city program (on-page, technical, clusters, and local). Most Edmonton businesses need both. Start here if the pack is the gap.

Do you work on Google Business Profile?

Yes. Categories, services, photos, posts, and review response are the core of this page.

How long does local SEO take?

Profile and citation work can move in weeks. Competitive pack terms take months. We date it after the audit.

Do you write service-area pages?

Yes. We write neighborhood and service-area pages that match how people search. The site build is website design if those pages do not exist yet.
