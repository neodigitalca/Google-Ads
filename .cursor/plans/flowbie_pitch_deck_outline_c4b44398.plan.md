# Neodigital and FlowbieONE: search deck (10 slides)

**Rules:** Each slide is an `##` heading. Four bullets per slide. Use **bold** for at most one short phrase per bullet, usually a product name or one outcome, so the slide is easy to read without a pattern of half-bold sentences. Slide 1 is the only intro. The rest is search and content in plain terms. No “Visual” lines. No em dashes. No en dashes in ranges (use “to” or “through”). **FlowbieONE** connects to your WordPress site. Pages and posts live in WordPress, and the program follows that.

## Neodigital and FlowbieONE (intro only)

- Neodigital plans and runs search work: which topics to own, what to publish, what to fix on the site, and what to refresh over time.
- **FlowbieONE** connects to your WordPress site so the plan, briefs, status, and reporting follow the same URLs, pages, and posts (live and draft) that are in WordPress.
- The next slides list what you get in search: better pages, stronger results, and reporting you can act on.
- Your main contact is Neodigital. We run the program in FlowbieONE on our side. It is **connected to your WordPress** install, so we are always working against the same pages and posts you have there.

## Planning topics and the buyer journey

- We group related searches and map them to a main page and supporting pages so your site **covers a topic in depth** instead of standalone articles that do not add up.
- We match content type to what people want at each step (learn, compare, buy) so the right page answers the right search.
- In **FlowbieONE** we compare the brief and target keyword to the page that is actually in WordPress and fix gaps in that order.
- This reduces two pages fighting for the same search and thin pages that hurt results.

## On page work we ship

- We ship page titles, search snippets, clear headings, FAQ copy where it helps, and structured data where it earns visible search results (rich results) for your market. **Edits go into WordPress** (or the agreed handoff).
- When it fits the search, we write so you can win the answer box and common follow up questions with short direct answers and a clean page layout.
- Page speed and indexing fixes are tracked on the same WordPress page they belong to, so technical fixes and copy stay together.
- If a page gets a lot of impressions but few clicks in Google Search Console, we start a **fix list** for that page instead of guessing from old exports.

## Local and maps

- We build location and service pages with real local details (who you help, work you did, local FAQs) not copy paste city pages that search engines devalue, published in WordPress the same as the rest of the program.
- We align Google Business Profile (categories, services, posts, Q and A) with what the website says for those markets so **Maps and the site** list the same services, areas, and key facts.
- Reviews and photos are part of a set schedule on profile work, not only when someone remembers to ask.
- When it is in scope, business name, phone, and links stay consistent across profile and site so customers and search engines are not confused.

## Types of content in the program

- In depth guides and how to articles to catch top of funnel search and link to your sales pages inside the site.
- Comparison and alternative pages for people who are shopping and not ready to buy on the first click.
- Service, pricing, FAQ, and proof pages for ready to buy search with a clear next step on the page.
- For large sites we use **templated pages in WordPress** with strict rules so you get scale without thin or duplicate pages that hurt the whole site.

## Keeping content up to date

- On a set rhythm we refresh high value pages (facts, stories, internal links) so the site does not **age out** against newer competitors.
- We rewrite search result snippets when titles and descriptions stop earning clicks for the rank you already have.
- We protect strong pages that already get traffic before we only chase new keywords and leave winners to slide backward.
- When a product, offer, or location ends, we set redirects and merges in WordPress so the site does not keep dead links and thin duplicate pages.

## Linking inside your site and fixing overlap

- We set parent and child links so key pages support deeper pages, and those pages link back in a way that is clear for people and for search engines.
- We vary anchor text so the site does not read like one phrase repeated, while still being on topic.
- If two pages fight for the same search, we merge, redirect, split the angle, or remove a page from search on purpose, **picked to lift the right page** for that topic, done in WordPress so the live site matches the plan.
- New posts and pages get inbound links from the right older pages in the same release cycle.

## What we report and what we do next

- We track rank by topic group and page so a move up or down ties to **a page we can work on** next.
- In Google Search Console we sort for impressions, clicks, and position to find almost there pages, for example strong views but weak clicks, or results near the bottom of page one.
- When analytics is in place, we look at organic traffic and leads to the same URLs we are improving in WordPress.
- Each month ends with a **short action list in FlowbieONE** with tickets tied to WordPress URLs, so a report is never the end of the work.

## Quality at scale

- We use approved templates and blocks in WordPress for many locations or many products so you get consistency and each page still has required unique text where it must.
- We use light QA rules by content type, such as minimum internal links, schema rules where we use it, and bans on thin filler.
- We send writers one brief with search term, what the results page looks like, competitor page links, and a suggested outline to cut rounds of edits, then the built page lives in WordPress after review.
- The **URL list in FlowbieONE** is driven from the connected WordPress site so you see what is covered and what is still at risk (weak, thin, or competing) in one view for the program.
