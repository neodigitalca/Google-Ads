# NEO Pulse program brief

## Product

NEO Pulse is an AI content and flow manager for WordPress SEO teams and agencies.

- **Product URL:** https://neodigital.ca/neo-pulse
- **Agency demo CTA:** https://neodigital.ca/
- **Core value:** Search Console signals become short action lists tied to WordPress URLs (titles to fix, pages to refresh, tickets ready to ship).
- **Audience:** WordPress SEO agencies, in-house content ops, local/programmatic SEO teams.

## Program modules

- **Overview:** Sitemap, page inventory, meta optimization, and content research tied to live WordPress URLs.
- **Bulk generator:** CSV and prompt bulk flows with approved templates, QA rules, and one brief per page.
- **Manager:** Properties, API keys, master rules, AI models, Google services, teams, tasks, and chat.
- **PPC:** Google Search and Meta feed ad workspaces grounded in WordPress page context and GSC queries.
- **Integrations:** WordPress plugin connects briefs, status, and reporting to the same pages and posts clients already have.

## Visual language (Instagram ads)

- **Background:** White or light neutral studio (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8F8F8`). No dark fills.
- **Accent:** `#84BC00` lime green as small UI highlight only.
- **Typography:** Lato. No logos in generated creatives.
- **On-image copy:** One short headline (max 6 words) plus optional 3 to 5 word subline. Body copy and captions live in Meta ad fields only.
- **Layout:** Photo-real Instagram feed/story sponsored post creative. Tangible props, devices on white desk, or UI screenshots on light backgrounds. Single focal point, generous negative space. Not infographics, charts, neon abstract art, or blog collages.

## Valid UI vignettes for product ads

Use these when art direction calls for product UI on white backgrounds, not generic SaaS mockups:

- **Action list vignette:** Short GSC-derived action list on clipboard or laptop screen on white desk.
- **WordPress-connected workflow:** Plan, briefs, status, and reporting UI on laptop screen (live and draft URLs).
- **Bulk scale vignette:** Templates, QA rules, and bulk page flows on white desk setup.
- **Structured AI flows:** Blueprints, agents, and WordPress publish path on light UI screenshot.

Reject unrelated SaaS dashboards, neon circuit graphics, dark gradient tech wallpaper, and generic stock laptops unless visual direction explicitly names hardware.

## Visual reference planning rules

When context is NEO Pulse app or creative mode is product_saas:

1. Always plan a **layout** element (Instagram feed sponsored ad on white background, minimal text).
2. Plan a **product vignette** prop or scene (action list on clipboard, WordPress UI on laptop, bulk scale desk setup).
3. Add **map** element when the ad angle involves local search, maps, or geo keywords.
4. Add **device**, **prop**, or **scene** elements when visual direction names hardware, objects, or environments.
5. All Google Images queries must target white-background product photography.
